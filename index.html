Grand Floridian

Disney's flagship Victorian resort on the Magic Kingdom monorail loop. White-and-gold architecture, a 5-story Grand Lobby with a live orchestra, Cinderella's Castle visible from the shore. Club Level concierge offers dedicated lounge with food & drinks around the clock. Best for the senior couples who want pure elegance.

→ Official page

Polynesian Village Resort

South Pacific longhouse theming on the monorail, with volcano-themed pool, Trader Sam's Grog Grotto (late-night bar), and direct beach views of the Magic Kingdom. A strong pick for families with little ones — the atmosphere is warm, tropical, and magical without being overwhelming. Also on the monorail loop.

Why Animal Kingdom Lodge fits this group

Rooms and villas look out over working savannas, so the 4-year-old, the 2-year-old and the grandparents can watch giraffes and zebras from a balcony without going anywhere. Disney lists 30+ species on the grounds. Both buildings have villas with full kitchens and washer/dryers, which matters with an infant and a toddler on different nap schedules.

Both Jambo House and Kidani Village have scheduled guest-room refurbishments that wrap up before your dates (Kidani by May 2026, Jambo by January 2027), so you should land in refreshed rooms.

The trade-offs to know about

Bus-only transportation. There's no monorail, Skyliner or boat to the parks from here, so every park trip is a bus. With seniors who move slowly and a stroller brigade, build in extra time and plan midday returns.

Animal Kingdom is mid-transformation. DinoLand U.S.A. closed in early 2026 for the new Tropical Americas land (Encanto + Indiana Jones). Disney says it opens in 2027 but hasn't given a date, so plan the trip as if it may not be open.

Where to Stay Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge · Jambo House + Kidani Village

Jambo House vs. Kidani Village

Jambo House is the original lodge with the big lobby, Boma, Jiko, the Victoria Falls lounge, Uzima Springs pool (slide, kiddie pool, hot tub) and Club Level (Kilimanjaro Club). It has standard rooms plus studios and villas. The bus stop is near the entrance, in front of the Giraffe Trail rooms.

Kidani Village is all villas, with its own front desk, Sanaa restaurant and Samawati Springs pool. It's a short walk or internal shuttle from Jambo. Both buildings list 3-bedroom Grand Villas.

My lean: put everyone at Jambo House so there's one lobby, one bus stop and one pool deck. Use Kidani villas only if Jambo runs short of 1-bedrooms.

Savanna views: required for every room

Every family gets a Savanna View room, with a balcony overlooking the giraffes and zebras. When you book, pick a room or villa category with "Savanna View" in its name. Standard, Value and Pool View rooms face the pool, the parking lot or the woods instead.

The Savanna View category is what gets you the animal view. Which savanna you face is only a request. Ask for Arusha Savanna at Jambo House or Sunset Savanna at Kidani. Savanna View costs more, so confirm with Disney when you book that the category guarantees a savanna-facing balcony.

For mobility needs, ask Disney about accessible rooms. Both buildings list wheelchair-accessible villas, including options with a roll-in shower.

Tickets & "Bulk Ordering" Straight answer: there's no group discount at your size

What Disney's group programs actually require

Disney's discounted group rates require booking 10 or more resort hotel rooms for at least a night, or a qualifying school/youth group. You're likely at 6–7 rooms, so you don't qualify. Disney's own planning team confirms that there's no bulk-ticket discount for a group of this size. The ticket price is the same whether you buy 2 or 20.

So the "key date" for tickets isn't about unlocking a discount. It's about buying in one organized batch, in time to link everyone before Lightning Lane day.

How to buy 13–14 tickets cleanly

  • Online checkout caps at 10 tickets per order. Make two orders, or call Disney Ticketing at (407) 939-7679.
  • Children under 3 don't need a ticket. Ages 3–9 are the child price. Age 10+ is adult.
  • Pick a start date of Mon Aug 23 for a 5-day ticket, and add Park Hopper only to the tickets that need it.
  • Link every ticket to My Disney Experience by about June 1.
  • Ask a free Disney-authorized planner whether any room-and-ticket package beats buying separately.

What the 2027 prices look like

Disney prices tickets by date and park. For 2027, single-day, one-park tickets run roughly $119–$189, and Animal Kingdom is typically the cheapest park. Multi-day tickets cost less per day. Park Hopper adds a premium: on a $119 day, the one-day hopper price was listed at $206. Disney raised annual-pass and peak-date prices on Oct 6, 2026, but multi-day pricing for late 2027 didn't change. Lightning Lane Multi Pass is sold separately and isn't included in tickets. Pull the exact prices for Aug 23–27 from Disney's site, since they vary by day.

Heads-Up & Open Questions Things that could change the plan

Weather & hurricane season

Late August in Orlando is hot, humid, and thunderstorm-prone in the afternoon, and it sits inside peak hurricane season. Keep 2–4 pm for indoor rides, shows, or the resort. Buy trip insurance for the group, ideally a policy with a cancel-for-any-reason option, and check airline and hotel change rules when you book.

Tropical Americas timing

Disney has said the new Animal Kingdom land (Encanto ride, Indiana Jones ride, carousel) opens in 2027, with no date. Some outlets think it could slip to 2028. Don't plan around it. If it opens before August, expect long waits at Animal Kingdom, and use a Lightning Lane Single Pass if you want the Encanto ride.

Mobility

Bus-only transport and big parks are the main challenges. Rent a wheelchair or ECV for the long days (EPCOT, Animal Kingdom), and ask Disney whether the Disability Access Service fits anyone in the group. You can register up to 60 days out.
