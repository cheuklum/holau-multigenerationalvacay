Grand Floridian

Disney's flagship Victorian resort on the Magic Kingdom monorail loop. White-and-gold architecture, a 5-story Grand Lobby with a live orchestra, Cinderella's Castle visible from the shore. Club Level concierge offers dedicated lounge with food & drinks around the clock. Best for the first_gen couples who want pure elegance.

→ Official page

Polynesian Village Resort

South Pacific longhouse theming on the monorail, with volcano-themed pool, Trader Sam's Grog Grotto (late-night bar), and direct beach views of the Magic Kingdom. A strong pick for families with little ones — the atmosphere is warm, tropical, and magical without being overwhelming. Also on the monorail loop.

→ Official page

About

Jamaica's most storied luxury resort — open for over 70 years, recently renovated and reopened April 2026 under Salamander Collection management after Hurricane Melissa. Set on 400 acres with two miles of private beachfront. Three distinct experiences: Eclipse (57 luxury beachfront rooms), Founders Cove (10 cottages), and the Rose Hall Villas (5–7 bedroom private villas).

The play for your group: Book one 7-bedroom Rose Hall Villa (~14–16 guests) with private pool, butler, housekeeper, and personal cook. Your entire group in one compound.
